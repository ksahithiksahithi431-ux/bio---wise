BEGINNER'S LEARNING CENTRE

Learn about biogas

Understand the basics before planning a plant.

📘

What is biogas?

Biogas is a gas mixture produced when microorganisms break down organic material in an oxygen-free environment.

⚙️

How does a plant work?

Organic feedstock enters a digester, where biological processes produce gas and a remaining digestate material.

🍃

Choosing feedstock

Food waste, animal manure, and some crop residues may be suitable, depending on their characteristics and plant design.

🌿

Digestate and slurry

Learn how digester residue may be managed and used appropriately after checking its quality and local requirements.

IMPORTANT

Biogas plant safety

  • Biogas can be flammable. Keep flames and ignition sources away from gas equipment.
  • Never enter a digester, pit, or enclosed gas space. Dangerous gases and oxygen deficiency can cause serious harm.
  • Have gas pipes, storage systems, and appliances installed and checked by qualified professionals.
  • Do not rely on this website alone to choose a plant size or construct a gas system.
